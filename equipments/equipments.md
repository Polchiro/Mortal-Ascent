# Equipment — Weapons

Weapons only give stat buffs. They do not affect which techniques can be used.
One weapon equipped at a time. Armor and artifacts come later.

## Tiers

| Tier | Name | Can equip from |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ⚪ Ordinary | Mortal |
| 2 | 🟢 Refined | Martial Apprentice |
| 3 | 🔵 Spirit | Martial Expert |
| 4 | 🟣 Treasure | Grand Martial Master |
| 5 | 🟠 Divine | Martial King |

## Types

| Type | Buffs | Split | Suits |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🗡️ Sword | Attack + Defense | 60% / 40% | Balanced |
| 🔪 Saber | Attack | 100% | Aggressive (no protection) |
| 🔱 Spear | Attack + HP | 60% / 40% | Bruiser |
| 🥢 Staff | Defense + HP | 50% / 50% | Tank (no extra damage) |
| 🥊 Gauntlets | Attack + Defense + HP | 50% / 25% / 25% | All-rounder |

## Drop rates

Weapons drop from chests (chests drop after defeating beasts).

### Chance a chest contains a weapon
| Chest | Weapon chance |
|---|---|
| 🪵 Wooden | 2% |
| 🥉 Bronze | 4% |
| 🥈 Silver | 8% |
| 💚 Jade | 12% |

### Tier odds when a weapon drops
| Chest | Ordinary | Refined | Spirit | Treasure | Divine |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 🪵 Wooden | 99% | 1% | — | — | — |
| 🥉 Bronze | 90% | 9.5% | 0.5% | — | — |
| 🥈 Silver | — | 85% | 14% | 1% | — |
| 💚 Jade | — | — | 88% | 11.9% | 0.1% |

### Average kills per weapon of that tier
(Elite chests: half Bronze, half Silver. Beast King chests: half Silver, half Jade.)

| Tier | From Elite beasts | From Beast Kings |
|---|---|---|
| Spirit | ~1 in 175 | ~1 in 17 |
| Treasure | ~1 in 2,500 | ~1 in 130 |
| Divine | Never | ~1 in 16,700 |

- A Divine weapon drop is announced to the whole server

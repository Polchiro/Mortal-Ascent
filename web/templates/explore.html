How fights work

The same fight as Discord
Strike beats Break. Into a Guard it is blocked and you are Stunned.
Guard beats Strike and counters for half.
Break beats Guard and staggers the beast. It stops charged attacks.

Watch the beast's warning: most turns it gives away its next move. In a team, each hunter picks a move every turn; anyone who hasn't picked within 30 seconds Guards. Setting out starts your 30-minute explore rest, shared with Discord.

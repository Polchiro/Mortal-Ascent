HOW TO PLAY

Meridian Circulation

The Qi keeps flowing while this is open.

The goal

You sit in meditation and look inward: your body becomes a map of meridians (glowing channels), and pulses of Qi flow along them. You never strike. You steer.

Gold Qi is pure. Send it into the Dantian, the golden orb in your belly.

{% if F.dantian %}{% endif %}

Dark Qi is turbid. Push it out through an exit: the white lotus on a palm or a sole. Never let it reach the Dantian.

{% if F.lotus %}{% endif %}

A session is three cycles of about 35 seconds. Pulses enter at the source points (the softly glowing dots: the crown, and at higher tiers a palm or a sole) and travel along the channels at a steady speed.

Controls

JunctionA jade ring with a number and an arrow. The arrow shows which way the next pulse will go. Click it, or press its number (1-8), to switch it. A pulse reads the junction the moment it arrives.
GateA red talisman seal{% if F.seal %} {% endif %}. Click it, or press Shift + its number, to close it: pulses stop and wait in front. Open it again and they flow on. A gate bursts if a pulse waits {{ G.gate.hold }} s or {{ G.gate.most }} pulses pile up: the waiting Qi is lost. A red ring round the gate shows how close it is.
BreatheHold Space (on a phone, hold the big Breathe button) to slow all Qi to 40%. It uses the jade Breath bar on the left: about {{ "%g" | format(G.breath.secs) }} s when full, refilling in {{ "%g" | format(G.breath.refill) }} s once you let go.
BlessingBetween cycles, press 1 or 2 (or click) to take a blessing.

Reading the map

  • Next (top middle): the colours of the next 3 pulses, so you can set junctions before they arrive.
  • The flowing dashes along each channel show which way Qi moves in it.
  • Round the Dantian: the gold ring fills as this cycle's gold arrives; the outer ring is your Stability (green, then amber, then red).
  • Top right: your score so far, the cycle (1 / 3) and your Harmony.
  • A knot{% if F.knot %} {% endif %} on a channel blocks it: any Qi that reaches it is lost. Route around it.
  • A channel drawn in red dashes is injured (Old Injury): Qi is slow there.
  • Between cycles the map shifts a little: a gate moves, a knot appears or moves.

Score and Stability

Your score (0-100%) is 60% gold delivered + 25% dark expelled + 15% Stability left, out of every pulse in the three cycles. With no dark Qi at all, gold counts 85%.

Stability starts at 100:

Gold lost−{{ G.stability.gold_lost }} (out an exit, or into a knot)
Dark into the Dantian−{{ G.stability.dark_in }}
A gate bursts−{{ G.stability.burst }}
A pulse handled cleanly+{{ G.stability.clean }} (never above 100)

At 0 Stability you suffer Qi deviation: the session ends at once and every pulse not yet delivered counts as lost. It never costs Qi you already have.

Harmony and blessings

Harmony counts the pulses you handle cleanly in a row (gold in, dark out). Anything else sets it back to 0. Every {{ G.harmony }} in a row is a Qi Resonance: your Breath bar is full again. Harmony never changes your score.

Open a meridian. When the first and second cycles end, choose one of two blessings for the rest of the session ({{ G.choose }} s to decide, or the first is taken):

Reward and rest

  • You gain {{ G.hours }} hours of your Qi rate × your score in Internal Qi, +{{ G.condition_bonus }}% for each condition, times any event bonus. The same as Discord's /train.
  • Under {{ G.low }}% you get only {{ G.low_rate }}% of that.
  • After a session your meridians rest for {{ G.rest }} minutes, shared with Discord's /train. The rest begins the moment Sister Jing seats you, so walking away earns nothing.
  • Breakthroughs and Spirit Dew happen just as they do on Discord.

Tips

  • Look at the Next strip, and set each junction before its pulse arrives, not as it arrives.
  • Two pulses close together, different colours, same junction? Breathe to slow them, or close a gate to hold the second one back, then switch the junction.
  • Open a closed gate before the red ring fills.
  • Gold is worth more than dark: if you must lose one, lose the dark.
  • Save your Breath for busy moments; a Qi Resonance refills it.

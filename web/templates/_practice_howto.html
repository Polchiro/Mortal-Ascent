Striking drills attack techniques

Hit each target as its ring closes. A gold target lights up on the dummy (the kicking post for kicks) with a ring shrinking onto it. Press that target's key when the ring meets it: right on it is Perfect, a little off is Good.

× 2

Pairs (palm techniques): two targets at once. Press both keys together.

1 2 3

Chains (combo techniques): numbered targets in quick order. Hit them 1, 2, 3.

Charges (heavy techniques, and every finishing blow): a burning target. Hold its key while it burns, and let go as the ring closes.

Small targets (finger and piercing techniques): the same, with tighter timing.

Defending drills defense techniques

Meet each blow as it lands. A sandbag swings high, a staff thrusts at the middle, a log sweeps low. A ring on your body shows where it will land: press that height's key when the blow reaches it.

Hold

Stances (brace techniques): press, and keep holding until the blow has passed. Letting go early costs you.

Tap

Deflections and steps: one well-timed tap. Your character sweeps the blow aside, or ducks and jumps it.

Counters (counter techniques): after some blows a ring appears at your feet. Press your Space move to strike back.

Breaths (recovery techniques): between blows, press your Space move as the ring of breath fills.

In every drill

  • Feints: a dark target or a dark blow is a trick. Leave it alone; striking it counts as a miss.
  • Pressing at nothing costs a little, so don't mash: wait for the ring.
  • The drill starts slow (Form), speeds up (Flow) and ends with a finishing blow.
  • Your score is your accuracy, plus a little for your longest unbroken run. Above 70%, she approves.
  • Change your keys and how much help you see in Settings.

# Privacy Policy: Mortal Ascent

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains what data the Mortal Ascent Discord bot ("the Bot") collects and how it is used.

## 1. What data is collected
- **Discord user IDs**, so the Bot can tell players apart.
- **Discord server (guild) and channel IDs**, so the Bot knows where to respond.
- **Game data**, such as your character, level, items, and progress.

The Bot does **not** read or store the content of your private messages, and does not collect real names, email addresses, passwords, or payment information.

## 2. How the data is used
The data is used only to run the game: saving your progress, showing your stats, and keeping the Bot working. It is not used for advertising or profiling.

## 3. Sharing
Your data is **not sold or shared** with anyone. It may be stored on hosting or database services needed to run the Bot, and is only disclosed if required by law.

## 4. Storage and security
Data is stored on servers used to run the Bot. I take reasonable steps to protect it, but no system is perfectly secure.

## 5. Keeping and deleting your data
Game data is kept while you use the Bot. To ask for your data to be deleted, contact me using the details below with your Discord user ID. I will delete it within a reasonable time. Removing the Bot from a server does not by itself delete stored data, so please contact me if you want it erased.

## 6. Children
You must meet Discord's minimum age requirement to use Discord and this Bot. The Bot is not directed at children under 13.

## 7. Changes
I may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

# Beasts

Beasts live in the wild and are met through /explore.
Each realm has **3 Normal, 2 Elite and 1 Beast King** — 66 beasts in total.

## Tiers

| Tier | Meant for | HP | Attack | Defense |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 🐾 Normal | Solo | ×1.0 | ×0.8 | Attack × 0.25 |
| ⚔️ Elite | Team of 2–3 | ×5 | ×1.3 | Attack × 0.25 |
| 👑 Beast King | Team of 4–5 | ×12 | ×1.6 | Attack × 0.25 |

- Multipliers are compared to a **player of the same realm and layer** (see stats.md),
  so the balance holds at every realm
- Damage uses the normal formula, including the 30% per-turn cap
- Each step deeper in an expedition adds +10% to beast stats (see explore.md)

### Example — Martial Expert, 1st layer (player: 380 HP, 95 Attack)

| Beast | HP | Attack | Player hits to kill it | Beast hits to kill a player |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Venom Scorpion (Normal) | 380 | 76 | ~5 | ~7 |
| Thunder Hawk (Elite) | 1,900 | 124 | ~26 (≈9 rounds for 3 players) | ~4 |
| Golden-Eyed Ape King | 4,560 | 152 | ~67 (≈13 rounds for 5 players) | ~4 |

## Roles

| Role | Change | Best answer |
|---|---|---|
| 🐗 Brute | HP ×1.2, Attack ×0.9 | Long fights — Recover helps |
| 🐺 Hunter | HP ×0.85, Attack ×1.2 | Strong defense — Counter shines |
| 🦅 Swift | 15% chance to dodge each hit | Combo (more hits, more chances) |
| 🐢 Armored | Defense ×2 | Pierce cuts through |

## Special rules

- **Charged attacks** (Elite and Beast King only): announced one turn ahead,
  e.g. *"The Tiger crouches low, gathering power..."* — ignores the 30% cap.
  Guard or take a huge hit.
- **Team size:** stats follow the team's highest realm and layer.
  Each player beyond the intended team size adds +20% HP.
- **Break the beast:** landing Break moves at the right time staggers it
  (skips a turn, takes extra damage).

## Lore notes

- Mythical names come from ancient Chinese folklore (mostly the
  *Classic of Mountains and Seas*, 山海经) and are free to use
- **Flood Dragon (Jiao):** a serpent that has not yet become a true dragon
- **Shanxiao:** a one-legged mountain spirit
- **Bifang:** a one-legged fire crane that brings wildfires
- **Tiangou:** the Heavenly Hound said to swallow the sun during eclipses
- **Qiongqi:** a winged tiger, one of the Four Fiends
- **Four Symbols** (Vermilion Bird, Black Tortoise, Azure Dragon, White Tiger):
  guardians of the four directions, met at the top realms
- **Kun-Peng:** a giant fish that becomes a giant bird — the final Beast King
  before the Heavenly Tribulation


# Exploration — /explore

Players venture into the wild, choose paths, and decide how far to push their luck.

## Basics

- Command: **/explore**
- Cooldown: **30 minutes**, starting when the expedition ends (safe return or defeat)
- One expedition = up to **5 steps**
- Not available during Seclusion or while injured

## Each step

1. The player sees three paths: **⬅️ Left · ⬆️ Middle · ➡️ Right**
2. Each path shows a **clue** about what lies ahead:
   > ⬅️ *You hear low growling...* · ⬆️ *A faint golden glow...* · ➡️ *Complete silence.*
3. The chosen path triggers an encounter
4. After the encounter: **go deeper** or **return safely** with the loot

### Misleading clues (by realm tier, see challenges.md)

| Tier | Chance a clue is misleading |
|---|---|
| 1 | 0% |
| 2 | 5% |
| 3 | 10% |
| 4 | 15% |
| 5 | 20% |

## Push your luck

- Each step deeper: uncommon and rarer encounters become more likely
  (step 1 ×1.00, step 2 ×1.25, step 3 ×1.50, step 4 ×1.75, step 5 ×2.00)
- Each step deeper: beasts get +10% stats (step 2 = +10%, step 5 = +40%)
- HP carries over between steps
- **Defeat:** lose 50% of the Lotus Coins and materials found this expedition,
  plus a 1-hour injury. Manuals and weapons found are kept.

| Encounter | Chance | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 🐾 Wild Beast | 35% | Fight a Normal beast of your realm (see beast.md) |
| 📦 Treasure Chest | 15% | Wooden 60% · Bronze 30% · Silver 10% |
| 🌿 Herb Patch | 12% | 1–3 alchemy herbs of your tier, ages rolled normally (see alchemy.md) |
| 🏕️ Peaceful Clearing | 10% | Rest: heal 25% max HP |
| ⚠️ Hazard | 7% | Poison mist or landslide: quick dodge challenge, fail = lose 15% HP |
| 🗡️ Bandits | 6% | Human enemies; lose the fight = lose 10% of your Lotus Coins |
| 🧳 Wandering Merchant | 4% | Sells a Qi pill and a 10-year herb of your tier for Lotus Coins (never buys herbs) |
| 🩸 Injured Stranger | 4% | Help (costs coins or HP): 70% grateful reward, 30% bandit ambush |
| 🪨 Ancient Stone Tablet | 3% | Mastery EXP for one equipped technique (2× a /practice session) |
| ⚔️ Elite Beast Lair | 2.5% | Elite beast — invite a team; the lair stays open 30 minutes |

| 🧙 Wandering Hermit | 0.3% | Special trial: big mastery EXP or a small chance of a manual |
| 👑 Beast King's Den | 0.2% | Server-wide alert; the den stays open 1 hour for teams to fight |

Blessed Land type: Spirit Spring 60% · Lotus Grove 35% · Dragon Vein 5%

## Chests from beasts

After defeating a beast, a chest may drop. In a team, **each player rolls their own chest**.

| Beast | Chest chance | Chest tier |
|---|---|---|
| 🐾 Normal | 30% | Wooden 70% · Bronze 30% |
| ⚔️ Elite | 60% | Bronze 50% · Silver 50% |
| 👑 Beast King | 100% | Silver 50% · Jade 50% |

## Chest contents

Every chest gives **Lotus Coins** (amount scales with realm — see economy.md later).
Then it may also contain:

### Manual
| Chest | Manual chance | Grade odds |
|---|---|---|
| 🪵 Wooden | 5% | Mortal 100% |
| 🥉 Bronze | 10% | Mortal 80% · Yellow 20% |
| 🥈 Silver | 15% | Yellow 70% · Mystic 28% · Earth 2% |
| 💚 Jade | 25% | Mystic 80% · Earth 19.8% · Heaven 0.2% |

- Earth manual: ~1 in 38 Beast King kills
- Heaven manual: ~1 in 4,000 Beast King kills (announced to the whole server)

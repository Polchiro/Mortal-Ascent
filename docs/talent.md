# Talent

Every player rolls one talent at /start. Talent is rare and shapes how fast you grow. It can only be changed by rerolling.

## Talent ranks

| Rank | Talent | Chance | Internal Qi Rate | Manual comprehension |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ordinary | 35% | +0% | Normal speed |
| 2 | Gifted | 28% | +10% | 10% faster |
| 3 | Genius | 17% | +20% | 15% faster |
| 4 | Prodigy | 10% | +35% | 25% faster |
| 5 | Heavenly | 6% | +50% | 30% faster |
| 6 | Saint | 3% | +75% | 40% faster |
| 7 | Divine | **1%** | +100% | 50% faster |

- About 1 in 100 players is born with Divine Talent
- **Saint** and **Divine** rolls are announced to the whole server:
  > 🌟 *The heavens tremble! A Divine Talent has appeared!*

## Rerolling talent

Talent is **locked to the Discord account** — restarting a character keeps it.
The only way to change it is a reroll, bought with Lotus Coins or premium currency.

| Reroll | Paid with | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 🎲 **Fate Reroll** | Lotus Coins | New roll **replaces** your talent — it can go down |
| 🌟 **Heavenly Fate Reroll** | Premium currency | New roll is **never lower** than your current talent |

- Both use the same odds as the /start roll
- **Fate Reroll** cost rises with each use: 10,000 Lotus Coins, then ×1.5 each time
  (10,000 → 15,000 → 22,500 → …)
- **Heavenly Fate Reroll:** fixed price in premium currency (set in the premium shop)
- Premium currency can be traded between players, so free players can get
  Heavenly Fate Rerolls by selling items for it
- Saint and Divine results are still announced to the server
- All prices are tunable once the economy is designed

## Changing fate — Heaven-Defying Pill

- Raises talent by **one rank** (e.g. Gifted → Genius), up to Divine
- Ultra rare: from the Wandering Hermit or Jade Chests (0.05%)
- Bound: cannot be traded
- Using it is announced to the server:
  > 💊 *Shoaib defied the heavens! Talent raised to Prodigy!*

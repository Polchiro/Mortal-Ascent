Stats — HP and Damage

Goal: fights are won by strategy, not by one-shots. Same-level fights last about 5 hits or more.

Layer bonus
+4% per layer (1st = ×1.00, 9th = ×1.32)
Peak (10th) = ×1.40
Base stats
Base HP      = Realm Base HP × Layer bonus
Base Attack  = Base HP × 0.25
Base Defense = Base Attack × 0.25
Buffs (weapons, armor, artifacts, techniques)
Final HP      = Base HP      × (1 + total HP %)
Final Attack  = Base Attack  × (1 + total Attack %)
Final Defense = Base Defense × (1 + total Defense %)
All % buffs of the same stat are added together, then applied once (e.g. +10% weapon + 15% technique = +25%, not ×1.10 × 1.15)
Attack and Defense come from Base HP, not Final HP, so an HP buff does not also raise Attack
Damage
Reduction = Defender Defense ÷ (Defender Defense + Attacker Attack)
Damage    = Attacker Attack × Skill multiplier × (1 − Reduction) × random(0.9–1.1)
Damage    = min(Damage, 30% of target's Final HP)
Skill multiplier: basic attack ×1.0 (techniques set their own)
30% cap: no single hit can deal more than 30% of max HP — no one-shots
Expected results
